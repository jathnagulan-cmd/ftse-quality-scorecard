    Has it worked?

    How it was tested, period by period

    At each date, every company was rescored using only the accounts published by then and that day's share price, then followed for twelve months, dividends included. "Top" and "bottom" are the best and worst fifth by overall score. The periods overlap and sit in one market, the universe is today's FTSE 100 (so companies that dropped out are missing), and trading costs are ignored. Treat it as a sign the quality scores aren't noise, not as proof.

    EquiSift is a screen for deciding what to research, not a list of what to buy, and not financial advice. Scores are relative: each metric is ranked against the other companies (margins and debt against industry peers), so 100 means best in the group. Quality carries three quarters of the overall score and price one quarter. News, results since the annual report and management changes are shown as Check first warnings but never change the scores.

    Data from Yahoo Finance, which has gaps and errors. Code and methods: github.com/jathnagulan-cmd/ftse-quality-scorecard
