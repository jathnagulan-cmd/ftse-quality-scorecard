FTSE Quality Scorecard

UK companies ranked on four things read from their annual reports: how profitable they are, how strong the balance sheet is, whether profit turns into cash, and what the market is charging for them. Banks, insurers and property companies are excluded because these measures don't fit them.

This is a screen to decide what to research, not a list of what to buy. It cannot see news, management changes or anything after the last annual report. Check the latest results before acting. Not financial advice.

How this was tested, and why it's weak evidence

At each date below, every company was rescored using only the accounts published by then and that day's share price, then followed for twelve months, dividends included. "Top" and "bottom" are the best and worst fifth of companies by overall score. The periods overlap and all sit in one market, the universe is today's FTSE 100 (so companies that dropped out are missing), and trading costs are ignored. Treat it as a sign the quality scores aren't noise, not as proof.
